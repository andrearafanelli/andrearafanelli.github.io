I am a postdoctoral researcher at [Amsterdam UMC](https://www.amsterdamumc.nl/nl), where I work in the [Translational AI Laboratory (TRAIL)](https://trailab.nl/) and the [Amsterdam Center for Computational Cardiology](https://computational-cardiology.nl/). My research applies artificial intelligence and machine learning to biomedical data, with a focus on cardiology and translational medicine: building models that can move from research into clinical practice.

I received my PhD in Artificial Intelligence from the [University of Pisa](https://phd-ai-society.di.unipi.it/students/andrea-rafanelli/) in February 2025. My thesis explored neuro-symbolic AI, studying how learning and reasoning can be integrated in agent and multi-agent systems.

During my PhD I collaborated with the [Department of Information Engineering, Computer Science and Mathematics (DISIM)](https://www.disim.univaq.it) at the University of L'Aquila and the [Department of Computer Science and Engineering (DISI)](https://apice.unibo.it/xwiki/bin/view/AndreaRafanelli/) at the University of Bologna. From November 2023 to May 2024 I was a visiting PhD student in the [Learning and Reasoning group](https://lr.cs.vu.nl/about/people) at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.
